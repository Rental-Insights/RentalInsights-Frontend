Buying a rental from 2,000 miles away usually goes wrong in one of three ways.

01

The wrong block

Two streets apart can mean tenants who stay five years or tenants who leave every year. Listing sites don't show you which.

02

Numbers that don't hold

Asking rents, skipped repairs and a guessed tax bill make a losing deal look like a winner — until month three.

03

A manager who disappears

Most reviews come from tenants. Owners need to know about fees, response time and how vacancies get filled.
